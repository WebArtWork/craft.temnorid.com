About the game

Build up your farmstead, grow food, gather the six resources of Lords (wood, stone, iron, water, grain and clay), and make tools, weapons and armour. Supply the settlements in Lords with resources.

Graphics are simple placeholder shapes for now. The game is built to be played first and polished later.

How to play

The first playable is the heart of Craft: a worker in a small settlement gathers six resources, carries them in a backpack and stores them in the warehouse.

  1. Walk around the settlement in third person.
  2. Stand next to a tree, a rock, an iron vein, a pond, a grain field or a clay pit and hold E to gather. A progress bar fills and the resource goes into your backpack.
  3. The backpack has 6 slots of 8 items (48 in all), and each slot holds one kind of resource, so you can carry all six kinds at once. When there is no room you cannot gather more, so one trip is never enough for the goal.
  4. Walk to the warehouse and press E to store everything you carry.
  5. Watch the warehouse totals for the six resources grow. Exhausted resources grow back after a while.
  6. Your progress is saved automatically, so the warehouse is still full when you come back.

You win

Store 10 each of wood, stone, iron, water, grain and clay in the warehouse. A message tells you the goal is reached, and you can keep playing.

You lose

Nothing can go wrong in Craft: it is a calm game about gathering and building up.

Playing with others

Up to four players share one settlement. Choose Play with others in the main menu: one player hosts and the others join with the address or code. The host owns the world (trees, rocks, ore, ponds, fields, clay pits and the warehouse); everybody gathers, carries their own backpack and stores into the same warehouse. A guest's progress is not saved, and if the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.

Phones

Android and iOS builds are made from Unity Build Profiles. On a phone there is a move stick, a look area (drag anywhere), and buttons for Use (hold to gather, tap to store), Jump, Run, View and Bag, with the places at the bottom.

Updates

The Windows game updates itself from the newest GitHub release when it starts. You can turn this off in Profile.

Part of the world of Temnorid

Craft has the same six resources as Lords: wood, stone, iron, water, grain and clay, with the same ids. Resources gathered in Craft will supply the settlements of Lords, and Craft will be linked to a player's Lords account later. Later, tools, weapons and armour made here will be used by heroes in the other games.

Today Temnorid Craft does not need an account and does not talk to Lords; you can play alone or with up to three friends.
