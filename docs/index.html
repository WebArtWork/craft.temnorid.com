Craft is about gathering, crafting and production in the world of Temnorid. The first playable starts with a simple loop: explore the village, gather its six resources, and fill the warehouse.

The wider goal is to build up your farmstead, grow food, and make tools, weapons and armour to supply the settlements of Lords.

An early look: the game currently uses simple placeholder shapes. Crafting tools, weapons and armour is planned for later.

How to play.

How to play, from your first harvest to a full store.

  1. Find something to gather

    Explore in third person. Find a tree, rock, iron vein, pond, grain field or clay pit. Stand nearby and hold E until the progress bar fills.

  2. Fill your backpack

    You have six slots, each holding up to eight items of one resource. Carry all six kinds at once, with room for 48 items in total.

  3. Bring it home

    Walk to the warehouse and press E to store your load. Anything that exceeds the stores’ capacity stays in your backpack.

  4. Head out again

    Exhausted resource plots grow back. Your progress saves automatically, so you can pick up where you left off.

Your first goal

Store 10 of each resource in one visit. That is 60 items, so you will need more than one trip. Stock starts at 750 of each resource; the goal only counts what you deliver. A message confirms when you reach it.

Take your time

There is no losing condition. When you reach the goal, you can keep gathering and building up your supplies.

Bring a few friends.

One village, up to four players.

Choose Play with others in the main menu. One player hosts; the others join with an address or code. Everyone carries their own backpack and delivers resources to the same stock.

The host’s village is shared with everyone. Only the host’s Lords stock changes. Guests’ progress is not saved, and the session ends for everyone when the host leaves.

Part of Temnorid.

Your work here can help your village in Lords.

Sign in with Google to play in your own Lords village and farm. Gathering in Craft raises the same resource stock you use in Lords. Craft changes your stock, without building or upgrading anything in Lords.

Playing offline, as a guest, or without a Lords save? You get a standard village, with resources saved locally in Craft. An account is optional.

Later, tools, weapons and armour made here are planned for use by heroes in the other Temnorid games.

Before you head out.

A few practical details.

How do touch controls work?

On Android and iOS, move with the stick and drag to look. Hold Use to gather; tap to store. Jump, Run, View and Bag are above the places. This page offers the Windows installer.

How does the game update?

The Windows game checks the newest GitHub release when it starts and updates itself. You can turn automatic updates off in Profile.

Where is my progress saved?

Your backpack and standard village stock save automatically on your device. In your Lords village, stock saves in Lords. Progress is not saved when you join someone else’s game.

Getting started

  1. Download Temnorid-Craft-Setup.exe and run the installer.
  2. The installer is not signed yet, so Windows may show an unknown publisher warning. If you choose to proceed, select More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Temnorid Craft from the Start menu and head into the village.
